The export tool runs in your browser. The personal access token you paste is kept in the memory of the open tab and is sent only in the Authorization header of requests to your Quip site, passed through a stateless relay on Cloudflare Workers. The relay stores nothing and does not log request bodies, tokens or document identifiers.

Exported documents are assembled in your browser and downloaded as ZIP files. They are not uploaded to us. To allow an interrupted export to resume, finished documents are kept temporarily in your browser's IndexedDB on your device until the ZIP is built; they never include the token.

We count a few content-free events (for example "dry run done") with a request that carries only the event name. Page visits are measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies. Payments are handled by Stripe Checkout; we receive the payment status, your Quip host name and your Quip user identifier to issue a licence that is valid for 90 days and held only in your browser tab.
